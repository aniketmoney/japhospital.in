Caring for Punjab's Joints & Spines Since 2013

JAP Hospital is Bhogpur's dedicated orthopedic and spine centre, built around one goal: helping patients of every age move, work and live without pain.

Our Story

A Hospital Built Around Movement

Founded by a team of orthopedic specialists, JAP Hospital was established to bring city‑level joint and spine care closer to families in Bhogpur and surrounding Punjab. What began as a small orthopedic clinic has grown into a full‑fledged surgical centre equipped with modern operation theatres, dedicated rehabilitation facilities, and a team that treats every patient with patience and respect.

  • ✓ Specialist‑only orthopedic & spine focus, not a general hospital
  • ✓ Elderly‑friendly facilities with wheelchair access throughout
  • ✓ Transparent treatment costs explained before surgery
  • ✓ Dedicated physiotherapy & recovery support after every surgery
Our Mission

Restore Mobility, Restore Independence

To provide safe, affordable and advanced orthopedic care so every patient can return to a life of movement and independence.

Our Vision

Punjab's Trusted Name in Bone & Joint Care

To be the most trusted orthopedic and spine care destination in the region, known for compassionate, world‑class treatment.
